1. What this site stores

VitaNord runs no advertising or tracking by default. The site works with two small entries kept in your own browser's local storage, and nothing else is set unless you allow it.

NamePurposeTypeDuration
vn.langRemembers whether you are reading in English, Norwegian or Spanish.Essential (local storage)Until you clear your browser
vn.consent.v1Remembers the cookie choice you made, so we stop asking.Essential (local storage)12 months

2. The map

The Google Maps panel is not loaded until you ask for it. Until then no request goes to Google and no IP address is shared. Once you load it, the embed is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can always use the “Get Directions” or “Open in Google Maps” buttons instead. Those only open Google in a new tab when you click them.

3. Changing your mind

Open Cookie settings at any time to change or withdraw your consent. You can also clear site data in your browser, which removes everything above.

4. Legal basis

Essential storage is used under Article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE), which allows storage strictly necessary to provide a service you requested. Everything else is used only with your consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

El mapa de Google no se carga hasta que lo pides. Hasta ese momento no se envía ninguna petición a Google ni se comparte tu dirección IP. Al cargarlo se aplica la política de privacidad de Google. También puedes usar «Cómo llegar» o «Abrir en Google Maps», que solo abren Google cuando haces clic.
