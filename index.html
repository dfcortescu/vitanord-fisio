Services

Treatments designed for every stage of your recovery

From your first assessment to your return to daily activity, we tailor every plan to your diagnosis, your pace and your goals.

General physiotherapy

Assessment and treatment of everyday muscular, joint and postural pain.

Sports physiotherapy

Injury prevention and recovery for athletes of any level.

Post-surgical rehabilitation

Progressive recovery programs following orthopedic surgery.

Manual therapy

Joint mobilizations and hands-on techniques to relieve pain and improve mobility.

Dry needling

Treatment of myofascial trigger points for chronic muscle tension.

Electrotherapy

Therapeutic currents as a complement to manual treatment.

Therapeutic exercise

Guided, progressive exercise plans tailored to each patient.

Geriatric physiotherapy

Tailored programs to maintain mobility, balance and independence.

About us

Physiotherapy with time, calm, and a clear plan

VitaNord Fisio was born from a simple idea: recovery works best when there's time to listen, a clear diagnosis, and a plan tailored to each person.

Our team of licensed physiotherapists combines manual therapy, therapeutic exercise and follow-up tools to support you from your first assessment until you're back to doing what you love, pain-free.
